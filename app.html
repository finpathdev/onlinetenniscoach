📹 For the most accurate analysis, film like this:
  • Camera angle: side-on to the court (standing at the net post or fence, facing the baseline), not from directly behind the baseline.
  • Camera position: mount on a tripod or steady surface — handheld footage makes ball tracking much less reliable.
  • Distance: far enough back that a full court line (baseline or singles sideline) is visible in frame — you'll use it to calibrate speed.
  • Lighting: film in good daylight or well-lit conditions; avoid heavy shadows across the court.
  • Clip length: upload short clips (a single rally or a few shots) rather than a full match — keeps analysis fast and accurate.
  • Background: avoid courts with yellow-green surfaces or backgrounds that closely match the ball color, since this can confuse ball tracking.

    How this works

    Pose tracking runs via MediaPipe. Ball tracking uses color + motion detection tuned for standard yellow-green tennis balls (works best with good lighting and a ball that contrasts against the court/background). Speed is converted to real mph using the calibration step, where you mark a known court distance once per video. Without calibration, speed falls back to a rough relative estimate. This is a lightweight CV approach, not a trained ball-detection model — it can lose the ball on fast blurred frames or busy backgrounds, and accuracy improves a lot with a clear side-on camera angle.

    🎾 About AI Tennis Coach

    A free, browser-based AI coach that analyzes your tennis footage frame by frame — detecting shots, estimating ball speed, spotting technique issues, and helping you track your progress over time. Everything runs in your browser except your saved sessions, which are stored on your account.

    📹 Filming tips

    • Camera angle: side-on to the court (standing at the fence facing the baseline), not directly behind the baseline
    • Position: tripod or steady surface — handheld footage makes tracking much less reliable
    • Distance: far enough back that a full court line is visible in frame
    • Lighting: good daylight or well-lit conditions, minimal shadows
    • Clip length: short clips (single rally or a few shots) work best
    • Background: avoid yellow-green surfaces that match the ball's color

    ❓ Common questions

    Is the ball speed accurate?
    It's calibrated against court dimensions you mark, so real mph — but accuracy depends heavily on camera angle and how well the ball tracking picks up the ball. Treat it as a good estimate, not radar-gun precise.

    Can it tell forehand from backhand for lefties?
    Yes — set your dominant hand in your Profile so classification works correctly for you.

    How does the AI know it's tracking me and not my opponent?
    After uploading, use the "Tag yourself" button to click on yourself in the video. The app locks onto your clothing color and position, and tracks you across the whole video.

    Where's my data stored?
    Video processing happens entirely in your browser — no video ever leaves your device unless you explicitly save it. Session summaries (stats, feedback notes) are stored on your account so you can revisit them later.

    Why do match records get deleted after 3 months?
    To keep your history focused and manageable. Practice sessions are kept indefinitely. If you want to preserve a specific match, screenshot the report card or export the session as a PDF from the saved sessions list.
